Holiday list upload

Holiday Date | Occasion | Holiday Type (Optional or Mandatory)

Reports

Reports keep the last 90 days. Older records are deleted automatically.

Daily rule: under 1 hr is not counted, 1 to 4 hrs Absent, 4 to 6 hrs Half day, 6 to 9 hrs Full day, 9 hrs or more Shift completed. Time after 9 hrs is overtime.

Salary

Salary is for the calendar month, 1st to last day. Today and upcoming days count as paid until you are marked absent. Mark days in Attendance.
